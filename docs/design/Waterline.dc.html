WATERLINE · UI CONCEPT · v1

Street-level flood depth, read from photos.

Night-storm instrument panel: near-black asphalt, sodium and cyan only as reflections, depth always in mono. Amber→coral is reserved for water. The best a road can be is “No deep water reported” in neutral grey. No green, no checks, no “safe”.

All imagery here is rendered, not photographed, and is labelled SIMULATION. Replay data shown is illustrative.
